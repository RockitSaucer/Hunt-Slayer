V7.0.38 Beta — Map search finds coordinates

What
- Map search bar accepts lat/lng and jumps there with the orange temp pin.
- Formats: 33.2098, -87.5692 · space-separated decimals · N/S E/W · ° / DMS · Google @lat,lng
- Pure coordinate paste skips Photon geocode (instant, no street-number noise).
- Enter jumps immediately if the query is coordinates (even before debounce).
- Placeholder: “Search pins, places, addresses, or lat,lng…”

Why
- Pasted GPS / OnX / Google coordinates never matched pins or place names.

Ship
- APP_VERSION 7.0.38-beta · shell v98
