Compass line click snap (reliable)

SVG stroke hits failed because cm-line-pane must stay pointer-events:none
(so the full-map SVG does not steal pin/dot clicks). Opacity-0 hit lines also
often ignore pointer events.

Fix: map-level geometric test — if a click is within ~30px (40px phone) of the
heading ray on screen, snap the measuring probe and skip the orange free-dot.
Also keep a near-invisible fat hit stroke as a secondary path.
