V6.6.6 Beta — changes since V6.6.5

Custom GPS / direction icons + compass mode
- Custom direction icons on your location marker now use the same rotation model as the default arrow
- Outer wrap rotates by device heading (identical to compass-mode line bearing)
- Glyph stays nose-up inside the wrap (frontDeg offset only) — no double-rotation
- Heading updates no longer drift the icon off the compass line
- Line continues to come from the front of the marker in the phone facing direction
