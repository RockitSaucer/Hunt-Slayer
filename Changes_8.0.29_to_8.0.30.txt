V8.0.30

GPS Track auto-stop
- Was: any single reading over 30 mph immediately stopped and saved the track
- Now: auto-stop only if speed stays above 40 mph for more than 3 minutes
- A GPS spike or a short truck hop no longer kills the track
- Dropping back under 40 mph resets the 3-minute clock
- Live HUD shows a Highway countdown while the clock is running
- Manual Stop still works anytime
