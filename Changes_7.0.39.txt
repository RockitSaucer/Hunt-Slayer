V7.0.39 Beta — Map weather popup close + setting

What
1. Fixed × on the compact map weather card — it could not stay closed because
   closeMapWeatherPopup() called closeMapWeatherDetails(), which immediately
   re-showed the compact card whenever lastMapWeatherPayload was set.
2. Settings → Defaults → Weather → “Weather popup when placing a map dot”
   - Checked (default): compact weather card can auto-open on free map click (Max).
   - Unchecked: card only opens after Get weather on a dot or pin.
3. Either way, hourly weather for the clicked lat/lng still loads into the
   dashboard / details (More details includes hourly). Wind HUD still updates.
