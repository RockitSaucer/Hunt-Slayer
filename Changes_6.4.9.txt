Distance from me / From location: yards until 1 mile, then miles

- formatDistanceFromMe(mi): under 1.0 mi → whole yards (e.g. 440 yd);
  at/above 1.0 mi → miles (e.g. 1.25 mi)
- Applied to:
  · Compass mode probe HUD (already used helper; threshold now < 1 mi)
  · Map-dot hunt status “from your From location”
  · Selected area distance (planner + weather dashboard)
  · Available hunts list distance suffix
- Path/measure lengths still use formatMiles (ft under 0.1 mi) — those are path
  length, not “from me”
